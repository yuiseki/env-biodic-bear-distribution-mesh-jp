# Goal of this directory

Build, verify and dry-run the publication of the bear (ツキノワグマ, ヒグマ) 5 km mesh distribution data of the Ministry of the Environment's
Biodiversity Center (環境省 生物多様性センター): the 自然環境保全基礎調査 動物分布調査 (do02, do04, do05, do06) and the 要注意鳥獣(クマ等)生息分布調査 (yo4).
Hub name (not yet created, tentative): yuiseki/env-biodic-bear-distribution-mesh-jp
Source: https://www.biodic.go.jp/shapedata/download.html
